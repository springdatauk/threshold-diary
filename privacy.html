Privacy Policy for Threshold Diary

Effective date: April 30, 2026

Threshold Diary is published by SPRING DATA LIMITED. We designed the app to be played without accounts, advertising, or tracking.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information through Threshold Diary. The app does not require sign-in and does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Local Game Data

The app stores gameplay progress, settings, found diary entries, best times, and purchase entitlement state locally on your device. This data is used only to run the app experience.

Data Retention and Deletion

Local gameplay data stays on your device until you reset progress in the app, delete the app, or erase the device. You can reset local progress in Threshold Diary by opening Settings and choosing Reset Progress. Because Threshold Diary does not create accounts or store gameplay data on SPRING DATA LIMITED servers, there is no server-side account data to delete.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple may provide purchase status information to the app so unlocked content can be restored.

Tracking

Threshold Diary does not track you across apps or websites and does not use the AppTrackingTransparency framework.

Children's Privacy

The app is suitable for a broad audience, but it is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

If you have privacy questions or deletion requests that are not covered by the local reset controls above, contact us at hello@springdata.uk.
