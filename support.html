Threshold Diary Support

Need help with Threshold Diary? Contact SPRING DATA LIMITED at hello@springdata.uk.

Purchase Help

If your full-story unlock is missing, open the app, go to Story Unlock or Settings, and tap Restore Purchases. Purchases are processed by Apple, so refunds and billing questions are handled through Apple Support.

Gameplay Help

Use two fingers to pan the paired images and pinch to zoom. Story Mode can be enabled in Settings for extra time and more hints.
